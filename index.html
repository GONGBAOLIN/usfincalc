Why USFinCalc

Every calculator here is designed to reflect real-world complexity. Where competitors stop at a bare monthly payment, we model the costs that actually hit your bank account: property taxes and insurance on a mortgage, state income tax on your paycheck, sales tax on a car, and federal plus FICA withholding on vesting equity. The result is a number you can act on, not a rough guess.

All calculations run entirely in your browser. We store nothing, require no sign-up, and never send your inputs anywhere.
