Calculator clusters

USFinCalc works best as a small network, not a pile of disconnected tools. Start with the decision in front of you, read the formula once, then move to the next calculator or guide that answers the follow-up question.

Income and tax

This cluster covers salary, withholding, RSUs, capital gains, and tax-advantaged benefits. The formulas are the familiar ones: marginal brackets for pay, supplemental withholding for equity, and basis plus holding period for investment gains.

Capital Gains Tax CalculatorCompare short-term and long-term gain treatment. HSA Tax Savings CalculatorModel the tax break from HSA contributions. Health Insurance OOP CalculatorEstimate annual out-of-pocket costs before enrollment.
Guide: Understanding Tax BracketsWhy marginal rates matter more than the top bracket headline. Guide: Take-Home Pay by StateHow state tax changes the number that lands in your account. Guide: RSU Taxes ExplainedThe 22% withholding trap in plain English.

Loans

This cluster starts with loan amount, rate, term, and any extra costs that lenders roll into the payment. Mortgage math uses amortization plus escrow items. Auto and student loans use the same payment backbone, with different inputs around taxes, trade-ins, and extra principal.

Guide: How Much Car Can I Afford?The 20/4/10 rule and the hidden cost of term length. Guide: Student Loan Payoff StrategiesAvalanche, snowball, and when extra payments help most.

Retirement and savings

Frequently asked questions

What is the point of the cluster page?
It keeps the next useful tool one click away, so a result can lead into the calculator or guide that answers the follow-up question.
Do I need to understand the formula first?
No. Each calculator shows the result first and the math right after, so you can get the answer and then inspect the assumptions.
Where should I start if I only have a vague question?
Start with the cluster that matches the decision: income and tax for pay, loans for borrowing, and retirement and savings for long-term planning.
